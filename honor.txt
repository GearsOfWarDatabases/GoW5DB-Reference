Honor / Allies system (from My Squad screen)
=============================================
Hovering a squad member shows an Honor panel:

  FPS PLEB
  NOT YET ALLIES          HONOR 0 / 25
  [progress bar]

  Campaign Chapters Cleared   0
  Escape Chapters Cleared     0
  Versus Rounds Won           0
  Horde Waves Cleared         0

  "Playing together increases your Honor"

Notes:
- Ally status starts at "Not Yet Allies"; Honor progresses 0 -> 25.
- Honor is earned by playing together; tracked per activity:
  campaign chapters, escape chapters, versus rounds won, horde waves cleared.
- Squad list: "My Squad (1/20) Friends Only"; menu also has
  Account Linking and Find Someone options.
